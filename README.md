Don’t Give Up on Love is Horizon Beats' debut five-track progressive house EP, released in March 2025. Built around themes of hope, devotion, and emotional resilience, the project explores the different stages of love through uplifting melodies, cinematic production, and heartfelt vocals. From the optimism of the title track to the timeless commitment expressed in Timeless Love, the EP established the emotional direction that would later define Horizon Beats' sound and marked the beginning of the project’s journey into melodic electronic music. The EP contains five tracks: Don’t Give Up on Love, Hold on Me, In Love With You, I’ll Be There for You, and Timeless Love
